## The promise

> **You run your business. We'll help you grow it.**

That's it. That's the whole pitch. Not "AI marketing platform." Not "growth suite." Not a feature list. A promise a business owner can repeat back after hearing it once.

---

## Why this exists

Small business owners are experts at their business and novices at almost everything the internet demands of them: a website that says the right things, a Google Business Profile that stays accurate, reviews that get replied to, content that shows up when it should, competitors that get watched, seasons that get planned for.

They don't have time to become marketers, analysts, or IT administrators on top of running the business. They shouldn't have to. That gap — between what a growing business needs and what a busy owner has time to do — is why AJN Marketing exists.

Project Magic 1.0 closed that gap for marketing. It made AJN Marketing feel like hiring a Head of Marketing instead of buying software.

Project Magic 2.0 recognizes that the same gap exists everywhere else the business touches customers, market, and goals — and that the intelligence AJN Marketing already built to close the marketing gap (understanding the business, its voice, its customers, its competitors, its seasons) is the same intelligence needed to close the rest.

**Marketing was the first room. The house is bigger.**

---

## What the customer should feel

The customer should feel like they hired a **Head of Marketing** — someone smart, proactive, and trustworthy, who's already learning everything about the business before being asked, who brings a plan instead of asking for one, and who gets better at the job the longer they work together.

The customer should **never** feel like they:

- installed software
- have to configure anything
- need to understand AI, data pipelines, or "connectors" to get value
- are responsible for knowing what to do next

If a customer ever thinks *"I need to go do marketing"* or *"I need to go check on my growth tools,"* the product has failed — even if every system behind the scenes is working perfectly.

---

## Focus: simplicity

Every ounce of complexity this product needs to be genuinely intelligent — data pipelines, connectors, scoring, forecasting models, competitive analysis, market monitoring — is ours to carry. None of it is the customer's to see, learn, or manage.

The measure of a good week for the customer is not "I did a lot in AJN Marketing." It's **"AJN handled a lot while I was busy running my business."**

---

## The one-sentence test

Before shipping anything under the Project Magic 2.0 banner, ask:

> *Does this make the business owner feel like their Head of Marketing just did something smart for them — or does it feel like homework?*

If it's homework, it doesn't ship as designed. Redesign it until it isn't.
