**Project name:** AJN Marketing ("Project Magic")

**Current product mission:** Make AJN Marketing feel like a small business hired the best Head of Marketing it could ever have — not like it bought another marketing tool. The product is evolving from an AI marketing platform into a broader "AI Growth Engine" for small businesses (Project Magic 2.0), with marketing as the first application of that intelligence.

**Current development phase:** Project Magic 2.0 (AI Growth Engine). Of the four planned waves in `docs/project-magic/IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md`:
- **Wave I** (Free Marketing Snapshot + Business Brain foundation) — shipped: public pre-auth Snapshot, secure SSRF-hardened backend contract, DNS-pinned continuation bridge, First Impression customer-facing UI, internal-alpha intelligence pass.
- **Wave II** (Connector Framework + Smart Uploads) — shipped: Business Connections foundation, Smart Uploads (PDF/DOCX/TXT/MD), Google Search Console as the first live Website & Search connector.
- **Wave III** (Customer Voice, Market Radar, Seasonal Intelligence) — partially shipped: Goals & Strategy shipped, Customer Voice Phase 1 + 2 shipped (Google Reviews + Website Testimonials providers). Market Radar: owner-managed persistence foundation shipped (`lib/market-radar/`, migration `037_market_radar.sql`, PR #101), the owner-facing view shipped (`/dashboard/market-radar`, branch `ai-queue/002-market-radar-view`), the Competitor Observation Engine (evidence model, confidence scoring, persistence — no UI) shipped via PR #107, Business Pulse Integration (Task 004) — a first, honest "What Changed" slice at `/dashboard/business-pulse`, branch `ai-queue/004-business-pulse-integration` — shipped, and **Weekly Executive Brief: Market Radar section (Task 005) — `marketRadarHighlights[]` on the weekly Executive Brief only, branch `ai-queue/005-weekly-executive-brief` — has now also shipped** (see below). Marketing Director recommendation-evidence integration remains unscoped future work. Seasonal Intelligence is **not yet started**.
- **Wave IV** (Business Pulse + Autopilot) — not started as originally scoped, but several features that read like an organic continuation of it have shipped outside the formal wave numbering (see below).

The 1.0 roadmap (`docs/IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md`, Phases A–H) runs in parallel and still governs marketing-specific delivery: Phases A–C are largely shipped; **Phases D, E, F, G, H remain scope-only, not shipped.**

**Task 005 — Weekly Executive Brief: Market Radar section: COMPLETE on branch `ai-queue/005-weekly-executive-brief`** (this update). Adds a genuinely new, additive field to the **weekly** Executive Brief only (`weekly_strategy_brief` — never the morning brief, never the monthly report): `marketRadarHighlights: ExecutiveMarketRadarHighlight[]` (`lib/executive-briefing/types.ts`), each entry `{ observation, whyItMatters, suggestedAction, confidence }`. `buildMarketRadarHighlights` (`lib/executive-briefing/buildBrief.ts`) is a new pure function — returns `[]` for `morning_brief`/`monthly_executive_report` or when `marketRadarObservations` is absent/empty; otherwise maps each of Task 003's confirmed `CompetitorObservation` rows straight through (`observation` = the signal's own summary text verbatim, `confidence` unchanged) with `whyItMatters`/`suggestedAction` copy derived deterministically from the observation's own `confidence` only — never invented from its free-text content, and falling back to a calm, generic-but-honest suggested action ("Review this observation before your next planning session.") for anything below `high` confidence rather than fabricating false specificity. No new persistence function, no change to `lib/competitor-observations/persistence.ts`/`types.ts` beyond calling the existing read function, no re-scoring of Task 003's confidence judgments, no change to `lib/head-of-marketing-orchestrator/` (`ExecutiveReview`, a distinct type). Tests: 8 new (`unit-tests/weekly-executive-brief-market-radar.test.ts`) plus one new test added to the existing `unit-tests/executive-briefing-engine.test.ts`.
