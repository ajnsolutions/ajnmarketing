**Project name:** AJN Marketing ("Project Magic")

**Current product mission:** Make AJN Marketing feel like a small business hired the best Head of Marketing it could ever have — not like it bought another marketing tool. The product is evolving from an AI marketing platform into a broader "AI Growth Engine" for small businesses (Project Magic 2.0), with marketing as the first application of that intelligence.

**Current development phase:** Project Magic 2.0 (AI Growth Engine). Of the four planned waves in `docs/project-magic/IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md`:
- **Wave I** (Free Marketing Snapshot + Business Brain foundation) — shipped: public pre-auth Snapshot, secure SSRF-hardened backend contract, DNS-pinned continuation bridge, First Impression customer-facing UI, internal-alpha intelligence pass.
- **Wave II** (Connector Framework + Smart Uploads) — shipped: Business Connections foundation, Smart Uploads (PDF/DOCX/TXT/MD), Google Search Console as the first live Website & Search connector.
- **Wave III** (Customer Voice, Market Radar, Seasonal Intelligence) — partially shipped: Goals & Strategy shipped, Customer Voice Phase 1 + 2 shipped (Google Reviews + Website Testimonials providers; Phase 2's "consume intelligence in Growth Advisor" scope is confirmed live in code — `lib/growth-advisor/buildGrowthAdvisorBriefing.ts`/`observations.ts`/`nextWeek.ts` all consume `CustomerVoiceIntelligence` — even though `docs/project-magic/IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md` still lists it as "next"; that source doc is stale on this point, not this file). **Market Radar is now fully shipped across all four of its originally-planned "How it surfaces" points**: persistence foundation (`lib/market-radar/`, migration `037_market_radar.sql`, PR #101), the owner-facing view (`/dashboard/market-radar`, PR #104), the Competitor Observation Engine (evidence model, confidence scoring, persistence — no UI, PR #107), Business Pulse's "What Changed" view (`/dashboard/business-pulse`, PR #109), the Weekly Executive Brief's Market Radar section (`marketRadarHighlights[]`, PR #110), and **Marketing Director recommendation evidence (Task 006, this update)** — see below. **Task 006 — Integrate Market Radar Evidence into Marketing Director Recommendations: COMPLETE on branch `ai-queue/006-marketing-director-evidence`** (this update) — the fourth and last of Market Radar's originally-planned "How it surfaces" points. `ClientRecommendationDecisionPackage` (`lib/recommendation-presentation/types.ts`) gains a new, always-present `competitorEvidence: ClientCompetitorEvidence[]` field (`[]` when nothing qualifies), built by a new pure `buildCompetitorEvidence` (`lib/recommendation-presentation/competitorEvidence.ts`) that reuses Task 003/004's existing infrastructure end to end: `listCompetitorObservationsForUser`/`listMarketRadarEntriesForUser` for retrieval, `buildWhatChangedItems` for relevance (drops an observation whose tracked competitor no longer exists), `filterObservationsByConfidence` for the confidence bar, and `lib/competitor-observations/confidenceLabels.ts`'s plain-language labels (never the recommendation-flavored ones — the exact mistake Task 004's own prompt already flagged and avoided). Additionally filters out stale observations (120-day cutoff, reusing `lib/marketing-memory/learningConfig.ts`'s `STRONG_PATTERN_MAX_RECENCY_DAYS` value as precedent rather than inventing a new number), defensively re-filters by `businessProfileId` (tenant isolation), guards against malformed observations, deduplicates to one entry per tracked competitor (most recent wins), and caps the result at 2. Wired into both `lib/recommendation-presentation/service.ts` call sites (`getRecommendationDecisionPackageForUser` and the batch `getRecommendationDecisionPackagesForApprovals`, computed once per business, not once per recommendation — no N+1) and rendered in the Approval Center's existing "Why this recommendation" disclosure (`components/dashboard/approval-queue.tsx`), only when non-empty. **Deliberately not wired through the `OpportunityCategory`/`translateOpportunityCategoryReasons` mechanism** (`lib/recommendation-presentation/reasonTranslation.ts`) — there is no product concept anywhere in this codebase linking a specific recommendation to a specific competitor, and inventing one would mean adding new opportunity-detection logic, out of this task's explicit scope ("do not redesign Marketing Director"); competitor evidence is presented as general business-level competitive context, never claimed as any one recommendation's specific cause. See `docs/CLIENT_RECOMMENDATION_EXPERIENCE.md` §4/§13 and `docs/project-magic/MARKET_RADAR.md`'s implementation-status note for the full reasoning. Tests: 15 new deterministic unit tests (`unit-tests/marketing-director-competitor-evidence.test.ts`) plus 8 new Playwright source-level wiring checks (`tests/marketing-director-evidence.spec.ts`).
